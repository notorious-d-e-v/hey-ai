“Hey Claude”

Claude opens in voice mode, ready to talk.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/notorious-d-e-v/hey-ai/main/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. Takes about five seconds. Free and open source, for macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon or Intel.

Up and talking in a minute

  1. Paste one line

    The installer downloads Hey AI, checks it, puts it in Applications and opens it.

  2. Allow three things

    Microphone, speech recognition and Accessibility, from one window. You only do this once.

  3. Say “Hey Claude”

    Claude opens with voice mode on. Talk. Say “stop listening” when you're done.

What you can say

“Hey Claude”Claude opens a new chat in voice mode.
“Hey Claude Code”A new Claude Code session starts, already dictating. End with “send it” or pause and say “enter”.
“Hey Chatty”ChatGPT opens a new voice chat.
“Hey Codex”Codex opens a new voice chat in your project.
“Stop listening”Ends whatever voice chat is open, right away.

It listens for names, nothing else

Hey AI uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice never leaves your Mac, and nothing is recorded or saved. The log keeps only which phrase it heard and what it opened.

While Claude or ChatGPT is already listening, Hey AI ignores wake phrases, so talking to your assistant never opens a second one.

Why Accessibility? Claude and ChatGPT have no "start voice" link, so Hey AI opens a new chat and presses the voice button for you, the same way a person would.

It needs the Claude and/or ChatGPT desktop apps. Hey AI isn't affiliated with Anthropic or OpenAI.

Questions

Does it work when my Mac is locked?

It hears you, but macOS doesn't let any app open other apps behind the lock screen. Turn on Keep Screen Awake in the menu if you want it to work while you step away.

Will it drain my battery?

It keeps the microphone open and runs Apple's on-device recognizer, which is light. Pause it from the menu bar any time.

Can I use AirPods?

Yes, but while any app holds an AirPods microphone, macOS switches them to call-quality sound. Use your Mac's built-in mic as the input to keep music sounding right.

How do I uninstall it?

Run curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/notorious-d-e-v/hey-ai/main/uninstall.sh | bash. It removes the app, its login item, settings, logs and permissions.

Why “Chatty”?

“Hey ChatGPT” is a mouthful, and short names are easier to hear correctly. Chatty is ChatGPT.
